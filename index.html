02 / OUR STORY 1956 — PRESENT

A LEGACY OF FLAVOUR

Rooted in
tradition.

A familiar name in the story of Kolkata's dining culture.

Mocambo has been associated with the city's distinctive restaurant heritage since 1956. Its identity brings together classic dining, memorable dishes and an atmosphere that feels connected to another era.

It is a celebration of the timeless appeal of good food and the experiences shared around a table.
